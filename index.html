The purple ones are the point of this. Their sprites are the real thing — lifted out of the .AD modules and decoded with the same routines the screensavers themselves shipped with, so the pixels and palettes are exact rather than redrawn. Behaviour comes from what the module says about itself: its settings, its sound effects and, where it has one, its own description string. See tools/README.md for how that is done.

The teal ones are the project this is forked from: After Dark imitated with nothing but CSS animations and transforms, no images and no JavaScript. They are other people’s work, so they are tucked away behind the checkbox rather than mixed in.
